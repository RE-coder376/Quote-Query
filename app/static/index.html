Asked to be forgotten?

There is no reply box, on purpose. Your team keeps answering in the WhatsApp Business app they already use. QuoteRadar only watches which chats went quiet.

How a chat gets flagged

Two things only: who spoke last, and how long ago. No keywords, no AI reading your messages, nothing to set up or tag.

Orange means the customer spoke last and you owe a reply. Slate means you spoke last and it's their turn.

About “seen”

A seen mark shows only when WhatsApp confirms one. If it's missing, we show nothing — a missing tick is not proof the customer didn't read it.

About the totals

Amounts are optional, so every total says how many quotes it came from. A partial figure that admits it beats a confident one that's wrong.

### Use a softer opener for inferred-fit leads

For businesses where pain is inferred rather than publicly admitted, do **not** say:

"I know your quotes are getting lost."

Instead say:

"Your business appears to send customized quotations. I’m researching how companies like yours track them after sending and whether open quotations become difficult to manage."

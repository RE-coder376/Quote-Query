Prefilled message:
AOA, it would be a simple tool for businesses that mainly sell on WhatsApp.

Its job would be to help you clearly see:
- which customer was already sent the price
- who replied and who is still pending
- which chats need follow-up
- which potential deals are getting buried or going cold

So instead of relying on memory or scrolling through many chats, your team can see pending deals in one place and follow up on time.

And no, it would not be free. But the idea is that if it helps recover even a few missed deals, the extra sales gained should be much higher than the tool’s monthly price, so the cost stays small compared to the value.

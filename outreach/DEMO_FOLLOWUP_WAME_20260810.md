## The message

```text
Morning — did that look like what happens at your end, or is it more the first enquiry that gets missed?
```

Why this one:

- Assumes they watched. "Did you get a chance to see it?" invites "not yet, sorry",
  which politely ends the thread.
- Asks about them, not about the product. Much easier to answer.
- Binary — answerable in three words while walking onto a site.
- Genuinely new information for them; follow-ups that repeat get ignored.
- It is also the diagnostic we still need: whichever half they pick tells us
  which side of QuoteRadar this market actually feels.

Do **not** apologise for following up, re-explain the product, mention price, or
send it twice.

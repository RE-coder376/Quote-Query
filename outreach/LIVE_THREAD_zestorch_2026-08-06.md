## What happened

1. **Us (msg 1)** — the standard validation opener: does any enquiry ever get
   missed or replied to late when the team is busy.
2. **Them** — answered **yes**.

That is a cheap yes. The question was leading; almost anyone says yes. It
confirms nothing about severity or willingness to pay. Treat it as *engagement*,
not validation. (Prior product: 7/120 "interested" that meant nothing.)

## Research done before drafting a reply

- **Middle East is the right channel.** WhatsApp B2B outperforms email in the
  ME/India/SEA — higher reply rates, faster conversations.
- **Length:** first messages should be 2 sentences, 3 at most. WhatsApp is ~95%
  open, ~4% reply — length is where it's won or lost.
- **Feature lists actively hurt.** Long pitches carrying multiple value
  propositions "read like marketing collateral" and underperform concise ones.
  → Do **not** list QuoteRadar's features in the thread.
- **Recorded demo converts 2-5%; live demo 6-20%.** The winning pattern is
  hybrid: recorded to educate, live for serious prospects.
- **A meeting request is friction** that filters out curious prospects who would
  have qualified given information first. → Send the film, don't ask for a call yet.
- **55% of replies come from follow-ups** — 2-3 spaced, each adding something new.

Sources: toflow.ai WhatsApp B2B sequences; outreaches.ai cold outreach benchmarks;
salesmotion.io 2026 playbook; guideflow.com live vs recorded demos; walnut.io
interactive demo conversion data.

## Proposed reply (Claude's recommendation)

```text
Thanks — that's exactly what I'm working on. I've built something for fit-out firms that lists every quote you've sent on WhatsApp, flags the ones that have gone quiet, and shows how much money is sitting in them.

Want a 1-minute look?
```

Reasoning:
- Two sentences + one ask. Matches the length evidence.
- **One** call to action. An earlier draft also asked "is it the first enquiry
  missed or the follow-up?" — dropped, because two CTAs split the response and
  the priority is getting the film seen. Ask the diagnostic *after* they watch.
- One concrete outcome ("how much money is sitting in them") instead of a feature
  list. Deliberately omitted, because they land harder in the film: no reply box,
  no AI reading messages, zero setup, won/lost, auto-reopen, wa.me handoff.
- "1-minute look" prices the yes at 60 seconds, not a meeting.
- Says "I build tools for fit-out firms" early — no hidden agenda to walk back later.

## Sequence after this

1. They say yes → send `demo/quoteradar_film_uae.mp4` (UAE edition, AED).
2. After they watch → **ask for ~10 minutes live.** The film is the qualifier,
   not the close; live converts 3-4x recorded.
3. Natural setup question at that point: **"is your business number on WhatsApp
   Business or normal WhatsApp?"** Consumer WhatsApp has no Coexistence path, so
   onboarding would start with an app migration. Better to know before promising.
4. If they go quiet → 2-3 spaced follow-ups, each with something new. Do not
   write off silence; over half of replies come from follow-ups.

## Do not

- Send the film unprompted.
- Quote a price before they've seen it.
- Claim staff-read tracking or "customer hasn't seen your reply" — WhatsApp
  provides no such signal (see `system_design/webhook_signal_verification_2026-08-06.md`).
- Ask more than one question per message.
